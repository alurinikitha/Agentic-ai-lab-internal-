Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly important in modern businesses. Companies use AI to automate repetitive tasks, analyze large amounts of data, improve customer service, and support decision-making. Machine learning allows systems to learn patterns from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed for every situation. However, AI also creates challenges, including data privacy, security risks, job displacement, and the possibility of biased decisions. Organizations therefore need to use AI responsibly by maintaining data quality, protecting user information, monitoring AI systems, and ensuring human oversight.
